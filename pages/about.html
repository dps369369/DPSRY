ABOUT DPSRY

A PLACE WHERE POETRY STAYS ALIVE.

DPSRY is a personal poetry archive and ranking system created to preserve poetry, understand it, and give every poem a place within a larger collection.

01

WHY DPSRY EXISTS

Poetry deserves to be preserved, revisited, compared, and understood as part of a larger body of work.

DPSRY exists because poetry should not simply disappear after it is written.

02

WHAT DPSRY PRESERVES

The DPSRY collection is organized around four connected elements:

01

POETRY

The central collection of poems preserved inside DPSRY.

02

POETS

The writers whose poetry forms part of the collection.

03

VOICES

Nine categories used to understand different poetic modes of expression.

04

RANKINGS

A system for comparing poetry and deriving poet rankings from the collection.

03

THE RANKING SYSTEM

Every poem inside DPSRY receives a rating. Poetry is then ranked according to that rating.

Poet ranking is derived from the average rating of the poetry belonging to each poet.

This means that the ranking system grows directly from the poetry collection rather than from a manually assigned position.

04

THE VOICE SYSTEM

DPSRY organizes poetry into nine Voice categories. Each Voice represents a different poetic approach or mode of expression.

LONGING TIMELESS WITNESS NARRATIVE PLAYFUL EMBODIED AGGRESSIVE PROPHETIC INTERROGATIVE

05

THE LONG-TERM VISION

DPSRY begins as a personal poetry archive and ranking system.

Over time, it may grow into a larger poetry platform where poets can create profiles, preserve their work, and publish poetry.

But the foundation remains the same: preserve poetry.
