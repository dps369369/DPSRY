ABOUT DPSRY

DPSRY is a personal poetry archive and ranking system.

Its purpose is to preserve poetry, organize it into a meaningful system, and rank poetry within the DPSRY collection.

WHY DPSRY EXISTS

Poetry deserves to be preserved, revisited, compared, and understood as part of a larger body of work.

DPSRY exists to create a personal system where poetry does not simply disappear after being written.

WHAT DPSRY PRESERVES

DPSRY preserves poetry and organizes it through:

  • Poetry
  • Poets
  • Voices
  • Rankings

THE RANKING SYSTEM

Every poem inside DPSRY receives a rating.

Poetry is ranked according to that rating.

Poet ranking is calculated from the average rating of the poetry belonging to each poet.

THE VOICE SYSTEM

DPSRY organizes poetry into nine Voice categories.

Each Voice represents a different poetic approach or mode of expression.

Voices are ranked according to the number of poems belonging to each category.

THE LONG-TERM VISION

DPSRY begins as a personal poetry archive and ranking system.

In the future, it may grow into a larger poetry platform where poets can create profiles and publish their work.

For now, DPSRY remains focused on building a strong foundation for the poetry collection.
